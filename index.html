Simple flow

From surplus to someone’s plate.

A clean four-step process keeps sharing simple: post, request, match nearby, and coordinate pickup.

1

Share

Post safe surplus food with quantity, timings, location, photos and pickup details.

2

Request

Organizations can post what they need, how many people they serve and where they are located.

3

Match

The backend checks nearby records and prioritizes suitable people within a 20 km radius.

4

Connect

Users can open WhatsApp manually with a prepared message and coordinate safe pickup.
